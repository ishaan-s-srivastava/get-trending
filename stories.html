Bean & Bloom Coffee — Reaching Students on TikTok

Location: Boston, Massachusetts

Business: Independent coffee shop

Campaign: TikTok short-form videos

Bean & Bloom Coffee wanted to attract more high-school and college students to its new location near a local university. Their previous social media posts were mostly reaching existing customers, so they wanted to introduce the shop to a younger audience.

Through Get Trending, Bean & Bloom searched for creators whose audiences were interested in coffee, student life, food, and local businesses. They chose a creator with approximately 18,000 TikTok followers whose audience was primarily college-aged.

The creator produced three TikTok videos featuring Bean & Bloom's iced drinks, study-friendly seating, and student discount. One video focused on the shop as a place to study between classes, while another highlighted three drinks under $6.

Within the first month of the campaign, the videos received approximately 74,000 combined views, 4,800 likes, and 350 comments.

Bean & Bloom also reported a noticeable increase in students mentioning the videos when visiting the shop.

Customer Quote

"We weren't trying to become famous overnight. We just wanted students in our area to know we existed, and Get Trending helped us find someone who already had their attention."

— Maya, Owner of Bean & Bloom

Campus Threads — Turning Instagram Views Into Website Visits

Business: Student-focused clothing brand

Campaign: Instagram Reels

Goal: Increase website traffic

Campus Threads sells affordable streetwear designed primarily for high-school and college students. They had an active Instagram account but were struggling to turn their followers into website visitors.

Using Get Trending, the company filtered creators based on fashion, college lifestyle, and streetwear. They selected a creator with around 27,000 Instagram followers whose recent Reels regularly received between 5,000 and 15,000 views.

For the campaign, the creator received three pieces from Campus Threads and produced four Reels showing different ways to style them.

One Reel featured a "3 outfits for a college week" concept. Another showed the creator putting together an outfit for less than $75 using Campus Threads products.

Campaign Results

  • 126,000+ combined Reel views
  • 7,200+ likes
  • 640 website visits
  • 96 product-page visits from the campaign
  • 23 purchases attributed to the campaign

Campus Threads decided to continue working with the creator after the initial campaign.

Customer Quote

"The biggest difference was finding someone whose audience actually matched our customers. We weren't just paying for views — we were reaching people who were interested in what we sell."

— Jordan, Founder of Campus Threads

Pixel Pop — Finding a Niche Tech Audience

Business: Phone accessories and tech products

Campaign: YouTube Shorts and TikTok

Goal: Promote a new product

Pixel Pop was preparing to launch a new line of colorful phone cases. Instead of working with a large general-interest influencer, they wanted a creator whose followers were already interested in technology, phones, and accessories.

They used Get Trending to find creators with audiences interested in those categories and selected a technology creator with approximately 32,000 followers across TikTok and YouTube.

The creator produced a series of short videos demonstrating the cases, including a drop test, a customization video, and a comparison between Pixel Pop's cases and standard phone cases.

Campaign Results

  • 91,000 views
  • 5,600 engagements
  • 1,100 product-page visits
  • 87 purchases

The strongest-performing video was a 24-second TikTok showing the creator customizing a case to match their phone setup.

Customer Quote

"We were a small brand competing against companies with much bigger marketing budgets. Finding a smaller creator with a very specific audience gave us a way to compete for attention."

— Alex, Pixel Pop

Fresh Start Fitness — Making a First Campaign Less Complicated

Business: Beginner fitness equipment

Campaign: Instagram Reels

Goal: Reach first-time fitness customers

Fresh Start Fitness sells affordable workout equipment aimed at people who are new to exercising. The company had never worked with an influencer before and didn't know where to begin.

They used Get Trending to search for creators whose audiences were interested in beginner fitness, home workouts, and wellness.

Get Trending helped them identify a creator with 14,500 Instagram followers whose content focused on accessible workouts rather than advanced fitness training.

Together, Fresh Start Fitness and the creator developed a five-video campaign called "5 Minutes to Get Moving."

Each Reel demonstrated a simple five-minute workout using one of Fresh Start Fitness's products.

Campaign Results

  • 58,000 combined views
  • 3,900 likes
  • 420 saves
  • 180 website visits
  • 31 product purchases

The campaign also gave Fresh Start Fitness a repeatable format that they could continue using on their own social media channels.

Customer Quote

"Before Get Trending, influencer marketing seemed like something only big companies could afford to figure out. The matching process made our first campaign much less intimidating."

— Sarah, Fresh Start Fitness
